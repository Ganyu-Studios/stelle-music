### 🐐 What is Stelle?

Stelle was created with the purpose of exploring, learning and using the possibilities offered by [`Seyfert`](https://github.com/tiramisulabs/seyfert), a new library to interact with the Discord API.

## 🔰 Features

Stelle packs a full music experience, and more:

### 🎵 Music

- 🎶 **PLAYBACK**: Play from multiple sources with search **autocomplete**, a full **queue**, plus `loop`, `shuffle`, `seek`, `move`, `volume` and more.
- ♾️ **AUTOPLAY**: Keeps the music going with related tracks when the queue runs dry.
- 📜 **LYRICS**: Live lyrics for the currently playing track.
- 📂 **PLAYLISTS**: Save and load your own playlists.
- 🕒 **24/7**: Keep Stelle in the voice channel around the clock.
- 📺 **REQUEST CHANNEL**: A dedicated channel with a now-playing panel and controls.
- 🎧 **MUSIC QUIZ**: A guess-the-song game — Stelle plays snippets while players race to name the **title** and **artist**, with a live leaderboard.

### ⚙️ Core

- ⚡ **NO HANDLERS**: Stelle wires everything for you, you just have to implement it.
- 🗄️ **DATABASE**: `Prisma` as `ORM` over `MongoDB`, with a `Redis`-backed queue store.
- 🌐 **LOCALIZATION**: Switch the locale to your liking (English & Spanish included).
- ⌨️ **COMMANDS**: Both `slash` and `prefix` commands out of the box.
- 🧊 **COOLDOWN**: Anti-spam on commands.
- 🕹️ **CONTROL**: Stelle provides a necessary (*that I consider*) control over the music.
